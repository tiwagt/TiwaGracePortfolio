Restaurant SaaS · solo build

PlatOS

A restaurant was running orders across WhatsApp, phone and walk-ins, and losing them in the gaps. PlatOS replaces that with one system: direct orders, menus and pricing, customer profiles, invoicing, and live remote monitoring of the floor. I built it solo, front to back, in React and Node. It now runs the day-to-day for a working business, moving several hundred orders a month through a single source of truth the owner can watch in real time from anywhere.
